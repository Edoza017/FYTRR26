PEAKFORM

Privacy Policy

Information you enter

PEAKFORM uses profile details, readiness answers, plans, workouts, nutrition and hydration entries, habits, avatars, and preferences to build and track your training. PEAKFORM saves these records on your device.

You can use PEAKFORM as a guest. If you use Sign in with Apple, the app receives an account identifier and any name or email address you choose to share. When email sign-in is available and you use it, Firebase Authentication processes your email address and password to create or verify your account. The app stores authentication credentials in your device’s Keychain.

Optional connections

  • Apple Health. With permission, PEAKFORM reads and shows steps, resting heart rate, and recent workouts. This connection is read-only.
  • Gym Map. PEAKFORM uses the location allowed by iOS to find nearby gyms through Apple Maps. Gym Friends and check-ins are saved on your device.
  • Calendar. With permission, PEAKFORM checks availability and exports the events you choose.
  • WHOOP. If direct WHOOP connection is available and you connect, the service uses your authorization to retrieve and display recovery, resting heart rate, and heart rate variability. The connection service retains authorization tokens to maintain access. Disconnect WHOOP in Health Connections to end the connection.
  • Exercise videos. Opening a video may load YouTube or another video provider and send the information needed to deliver the video. The provider’s privacy policy applies to its service.

You can manage device permissions in iOS Settings. Sharing a plan or exporting an event sends the copy you select to the receiving app or service. PEAKFORM does not use health or fitness information for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Signing out keeps your saved PEAKFORM records on the device. To remove your records, open Account in Profile and choose Delete account or Erase guest data. Deleting an email account also removes the email authentication account. If you signed in with Apple, PEAKFORM shows how to revoke app access in your Apple Account settings.

Manage records already in Apple Health, shared copies, and exported calendar events in the apps that hold them.
