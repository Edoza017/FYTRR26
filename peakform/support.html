Contact PEAKFORM

Email edoza017@gmail.com and tell us what happened. Include your device model, iOS version, and app version if you know them. Keep health answers, passwords, and other private details out of the message.

Using the beta? You can also send feedback from PEAKFORM’s TestFlight page. Open the TestFlight invite.

Find your training

Use Quick Start to build a starter week. Open Home to find today’s workout, then use Track to see what you saved. Log a working set or actual cardio time to save a completed session.

Manage your data

Open Profile and find Account. Choose Delete account if signed in or Erase guest data if using the app as a guest. The confirmation explains what will be removed.
