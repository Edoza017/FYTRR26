FAQ

Before you start.

Need help with your plan, account, or training history? Visit PEAKFORM Support.

How do I install the beta?

Open the invite on your iPhone or iPad. If you need the TestFlight app, Apple will show you how to install it. Then accept the invite and install PEAKFORM.

Do I need an account?

You can choose Continue without account after Quick Start. Your training plan and workouts are saved on the device.

Can I change the workout I’m scheduled to do?

Yes. Move a session in Weekly Planner, swap exercises from your library, or use Shuffle Workout when you want to train something different.

How do I send feedback?

Open PEAKFORM in TestFlight and choose Send Beta Feedback. Include what you were trying to do and what happened, keeping personal information out of your report.
